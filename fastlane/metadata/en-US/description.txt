Track one player’s basketball or soccer stats in real time with Stattie. Built for parents, Stattie makes it simple to follow your child’s game with large tap targets, shift-based stat capture, and instant post-game summaries.

Record shots, rebounds, assists, steals, fouls, turnovers, and sport-specific events while the game is happening. Review your player’s game summaries and trends, then choose when to share a completed-game stat summary with friends and family.

Features:
- Parent-focused tracking for one player
- Live in-game stat tracking for basketball and soccer
- Player and game summaries with season trends
- Shift tracking with score context
- User-initiated sharing of completed-game stat summaries
- iCloud sync across your devices
- Offline-friendly capture during games

Stattie gives parents a clear record of their player’s games and progress without distracting from the action.
