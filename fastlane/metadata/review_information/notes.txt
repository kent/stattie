No login or demo account is required. Core functionality is available immediately after completing the short on-device setup.

Review path:
1. Add a player from the Players tab.
2. Create a game for that player.
3. Track several made/missed shots or other stats.
4. End the game to open its summary.
5. Review the on-device next-game coaching focus and player trends.

Technical notes:
- Coaching recommendations are generated locally on the device; player and game data is not sent to an AI service.
- Stattie stores data locally and can privately sync it between the reviewer's own devices through their iCloud account.
- iCloud is optional for the core tracking and summary flow.
