No login or demo account is required. Core functionality is available immediately after completing the short on-device setup.

Review path:
1. Add the player you want to track.
2. Create a game for that player.
3. Track several made/missed shots or other stats.
4. End the game to open its summary.
5. Review the player’s completed-game stats and trend history.
6. Optionally share the completed-game stat summary with a friend or family member using the iOS share sheet.

Technical notes:
- Player and game data is stored locally and is not sent to an analytics or AI service.
- Stattie can privately sync data between the reviewer’s own devices through their iCloud account.
- iCloud is optional for the core tracking and summary flow.
